# Skill: Multi-Source Research

## Catalog description

Proportional workflow for current, technical, comparative, quantitative, or disputed claims.

## Purpose

Gather and synthesize external evidence without overstating certainty.

## Load when

- current facts, prices, availability, specifications, laws, statistics, rankings, or recommendations matter;
- sources may conflict;
- two or more sources materially improve confidence.

## Do not load when

- translating, rewriting, brainstorming, or calculating from supplied facts;
- the answer does not depend on external verification.

## Dependencies

Available web search and fetch tools when external evidence is required.

## Workflow

1. Define the exact question and scope.
2. Identify sub-questions and evidence requirements.
3. Search from more than one angle when appropriate.
4. Prefer primary, official, academic, standards, manufacturer, or institutional sources.
5. Fetch and inspect relevant pages; do not rely on snippets alone.
6. Compare dates, methods, scope, and applicability.
7. Look for counter-evidence when consequences are material.
8. Separate sourced facts, calculations, estimates, assumptions, and recommendations.
9. Cite important claims and state gaps or conflicts.

## Forbidden behavior

Do not fabricate citations, hide conflicts, present vendor claims as independent evidence, or make a confident recommendation from insufficient evidence.

## Verification

Check source relevance, authority, recency, contradiction handling, and whether the evidence supports the strength of the conclusion.

## Output contract

Use concise sections for conclusion, evidence, uncertainty and limitations, and recommendation when applicable.
