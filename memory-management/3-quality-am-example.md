### Key Patterns Demonstrated

1. **Progressive disclosure** — full gear list is in
   `photography_journey.md`, AM only carries the anchor.
2. **Trigger descriptions** — every Archive Index entry ends with
   "**Load when:** …" so the agent knows when to file_read.
3. **Privacy boundary** — contact info and private links are stated
   to exist but not displayed in this public example.
4. **One source of truth** — hobby anchor lives in AM, detail lives
   in file. No duplication.
5. **Behavior guardrails** — "do not want" section is concrete and
   actionable, not vague.
6. **Compact** — entire example fits in roughly 350 tokens, well
   under the 1500-token performance cliff.
